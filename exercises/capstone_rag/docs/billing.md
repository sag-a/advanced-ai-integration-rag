# Billing and Payments

## Payment methods
Customers can pay by debit card, credit card or bank transfer. Cash payments are
accepted only in our shops.

## Duplicate charges
If you see two charges for the same purchase, check whether one is a temporary card
authorization. Temporary authorizations disappear within 7 days. If both charges are
completed, contact the billing team with the dates and amounts of both payments.

## Late payments

### Late fee
A late fee of 5 euros is added when a bill is not paid within 10 days of its due date.

### Exceptions
The late fee is waived once per calendar year if you ask the billing team.
