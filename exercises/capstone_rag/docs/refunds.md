# Refund Policy

## Full refunds

### Eligibility
Customers can receive a full refund if they cancel within 14 days of purchase.

### Exceptions
Customers who have already used the service are not eligible for a full refund.
Promotional bundles bought at a discount of 50% or more can never be refunded in full.

## Partial refunds
After 14 days, customers receive a partial refund for the unused full months of a
prepaid plan. The current month is not refunded.

## Refund timing
Approved refunds are paid to the original payment method within 5 business days.
