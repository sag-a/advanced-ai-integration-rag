# Internet Support

## Router lights
A green light means the connection is working. A blinking orange light means the
router is starting or updating; wait five minutes. A red light means there is no
connection to our network.

## Wi-Fi troubleshooting
First, check that Wi-Fi is enabled on the router and on your device. Second, move
closer to the router, because walls and floors weaken the signal. Third, restart the
router by unplugging it for 30 seconds. Fourth, check that you selected the correct
network name and entered the password printed on the router label. Fifth, if several
devices lose Wi-Fi at the same time, connect one device with a cable. If the cable
works but Wi-Fi does not, the router's Wi-Fi radio may be faulty and the router can be
replaced free of charge. If the cable also fails, report an outage.

## Outages
Planned maintenance is announced by SMS at least 48 hours in advance. During an
outage, the status page shows the expected repair time for your area.
