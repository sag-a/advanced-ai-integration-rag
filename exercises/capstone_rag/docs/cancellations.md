# Cancelling a Contract

## How to cancel
Submit a cancellation request through your online account or in any of our shops.
You receive a confirmation email with a cancellation number.

## Notice period

### Rule
Contracts end 30 days after the cancellation request.

### Exceptions
Customers who move abroad can end the contract immediately by sending proof of their
new address.

## Returning equipment
Rented routers must be returned within 14 days after the contract ends. A router that
is not returned is charged at 60 euros.
