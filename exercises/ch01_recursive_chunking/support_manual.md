# Customer Support Manual

## Refunds

### Eligibility
Customers can receive a full refund if they cancel within 14 days of purchase.

### Exceptions
Customers who have already used the service are not eligible for a full refund.

## Cancellations

### Steps
Submit a cancellation request through your online account. After 14 days, cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period.

## Payments

### Methods
Customers can pay by debit card, credit card, or bank transfer.
