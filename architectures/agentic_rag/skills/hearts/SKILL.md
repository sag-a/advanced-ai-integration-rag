# Loyalty point research

Use `search_pdf` for the exact question. Read clause labels discovered in results
with `read_clause`, never infer facts from labels. Separate earning, permitted uses,
restrictions and expiry. Check explicit negative wording rather than generalizing
from a nearby positive statement. Preserve the PDF's calendar rule verbatim when
interpreting expiry; do not replace it with an approximate interval. Identify the
program, acquisition date and applicable version before combining results.

Do not infer the user's actual balance, current promotions, or redemption rights.
Stop when the relevant original passages cover the question. Use a brief
`assess_evidence` checklist to identify missing conditions. Return chunk IDs and
gaps; skills supply a research method, not authoritative facts.
